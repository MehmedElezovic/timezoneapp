Three things to try

  • Drag the skyor the line on the planner to look at another hour. Everyone moves with it.
  • Click a free blockon someone's row to book a time. Noon warns you before you book too close to their sleep.
  • Leave a note on any blockcomments, reactions and @mentions. Requests and threads collect under the planner.
