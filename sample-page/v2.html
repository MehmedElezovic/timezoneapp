The hidden cost nobody tracks

A remake is not one lost appointment. It is a second prep check, a second seat, and a patient who starts wondering whether they picked the right dentist.

  • Two extra visits for every remake
  • 38 minutes of chair time each visit
  • One in nine patients who never book their next cleaning

What the best practices do differently

They stopped treating fit as luck. They send scans instead of impressions, they get a design preview before anything is milled, and they work with a lab that owns the remake instead of arguing about it.

"We went from four remakes a month to one. I didn't change how I prep. I changed who I send it to." – Dr. Alicia Moreno, Moreno Family Dental
